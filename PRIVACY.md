# Privacy Policy — Copy Selected Tab URLs

_Effective 12 October 2026_

Copy Selected Tab URLs does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Tab URLs** (`tabs` permission): when you click the extension or press its shortcut, it reads the URLs of the tabs you have selected in the current window.
- **Clipboard** (`clipboardWrite` permission): it writes those URLs to your clipboard, one per line. It never reads your clipboard.

## What happens to that data

The URLs go only to your local clipboard. Nothing is saved by the extension, and nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and contains no remotely hosted code.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published in this file, in the extension's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/mawildoer/copy-tab-urls).
